Food that rots in landfill releases methane, a greenhouse gas far more potent than CO₂. Using what you buy is the simplest way to cut it.

Three storage habits that save food

Small changes in how you store things make food last longer.

  • First in, first out

    Put older items at the front of the shelf so you reach for them first.

  • Keep ripeners away from greens

    Bananas, apples and tomatoes give off a ripening gas that makes leafy vegetables spoil faster, so store them apart.

  • Use the freezer as a safety net

    If meat or bread will not be used this week, freeze it right away.
